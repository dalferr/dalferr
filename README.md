## 💫 About me

I'm a Computer Engineer from the Basque Country focused on **offensive security**. I like understanding systems from the bottom up — kernels, network protocols, parallel programs — and then figuring out how they break.

- 🔬 **Now:** Cybersecurity & Reliability Methods Intern at [IKERLAN](https://www.ikerlan.es), a technology research centre in Mondragón.
- 📚 **Studying:** MSc in Data Analysis, Cybersecurity & Cloud Computing at Mondragon Unibertsitatea.
- 🛡️ **Practising:** pentesting labs on Hack The Box and CTF competitions.
- 🤝 **Open to:** collaborating on security projects, CTF teams and research in offensive security.
